About ATB Group

Building Qatar's Infrastructure Since 2008

ATB Group is a leading construction and civil engineering company in Qatar, specializing in road construction, building projects, and traffic management solutions. As an Ashghal-approved contractor, we deliver exceptional infrastructure projects that meet international quality standards.

From highway construction and road opening permits to TMA rental, road marking, interlock installation, and complete civil works, our expertise spans every aspect of construction and infrastructure development in Qatar.

Our Mission

To build world-class infrastructure that powers Qatar's growth while maintaining the highest safety and quality standards.

Our Vision

To be the Middle East's most trusted construction and infrastructure development partner.

Our Construction Services

Complete Infrastructure Solutions

From road construction and building projects to traffic management and civil engineering, we provide comprehensive construction services tailored to Qatar's infrastructure needs.

Temporary Traffic Management & TMA Rental

Professional traffic control and Truck Mounted Attenuator (TMA) rental services to ensure worker safety and maintain uninterrupted traffic flow on construction sites.

  • STMS/STMP certified personnel
  • 24/7 TMA availability

Road Opening (RO) Permit Services

Fast-track Ashghal QPRO Road Space Booking and RO Permit applications with guaranteed accuracy and minimal delays for your construction projects.

  • Ashghal QPRO expertise
  • Hassle-free approval process

Traffic Diversion Plan (TDP) Service

Precision TDP design and drawing services fully compliant with Ashghal's WZTMG, delivered by certified A-Class designers with extensive MOI approval experience.

  • A-Class certified designers
  • Full WZTMG compliance

School Zone Safety Development

Complete school zone safety solutions from design approvals to on-site implementation, meeting all Ashghal Design Department requirements for educational institutions.

  • Child-focused safety design
  • Full turnkey implementation

Road Marking Services

High-performance thermoplastic road marking, two-component cold plastic paint, cycle track coating, and professional marking removal for maximum durability and visibility.

  • Premium thermoplastic materials
  • High-visibility standards

Traffic Signage Solutions

Professional supply and installation of permanent and temporary traffic signs, designed and implemented to Ashghal standards for clear, effective road communication.

  • Reflective grade materials
  • Permanent & temporary options

Interlock & Kerb Stone Works

Premium interlock and kerb stone installation services using superior-quality, long-lasting materials for durable roadside infrastructure and aesthetic finishes.

  • Premium quality materials
  • Expert installation teams

Vehicle Restraint Systems (VRS)

Supply and installation of safety barriers, crash cushions, guard rails, fish tails, and terminal systems for maximum roadside protection and accident mitigation.

  • Crash-tested systems
  • Full compliance standards

Construction & Traffic Consultation

Expert construction and traffic management consultancy for planning, supervision, and site coordination, delivered by experienced engineers and certified professionals.

  • Certified construction experts
  • 24/7 project support
